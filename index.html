Getting there

Flights

Typical lowest (saver) prices, collected in 2026 from published award charts and points-travel sites. Saver seats are limited and prices change. Find a seat on the airline's site before you transfer points, because transfers usually can't be undone. Taxes and fees are estimates.

Where to stay

Hotels

Card values: Capital One 1¢ per mile on Capital One Travel, or when you cover a travel purchase within 90 days. Chase 1¢, or up to 2¢ on select hotels with Points Boost. Amex 0.7¢ for hotels on Amex Travel. Citi and Wells Fargo 1¢. Bilt 1.25¢ on Bilt Travel. Stays booked through a card's travel site usually don't earn hotel points or elite perks.

Getting around

Rental cars

Rental companies don't take card points directly. You pay through your card's travel site or cover the charge afterward. Pay with a card that has primary coverage to skip the counter insurance.
